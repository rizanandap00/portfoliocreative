How I Work

Creative Process

A structured approach ensuring every commissioned artwork meets client expectations.

01

Brief & Ideation

Discussing mood boards, references, dimensions, and visual theme requirements.

02

Rough Sketch

Creating composition thumbnails and pose sketches for client feedback and approval.

03

Lineart & Color

Clean lineart pass, base flat colors, and establishing key lighting ambience.

04

Rendering & Delivery

Final shading, highlight FX polish, and delivering high-res CMYK/RGB files.

About The Artist

Rizky Ananda Pratama

Hello! I'm Rizky Ananda Pratama (online as rizanandap00), a passionate digital illustrator based in Indonesia. I specialize in character artwork, fantasy scenes, and narrative illustration.

My creative goal is to craft memorable visuals that convey emotion and spark curiosity. Whether working on personal IP development or collaborating with authors, game developers, and private collectors, I aim for visual excellence.
